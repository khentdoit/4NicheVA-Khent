Five portfolio builds

What each workflow solves.

Lead capture → auto-tag → CRM entry

A form submission gets tagged and dropped into a free-tier CRM (HubSpot or Airtable) with zero manual data entry.

AI email triage

Inbox messages get classified by urgency through the Claude/GPT API and routed to the right Slack or Discord channel.

Form → sheet → auto-generated PDF report

A submission logs to Google Sheets and immediately produces a formatted PDF report — no copy-pasting.

"New content published" → auto-post

One publish event fans out to two or three social platforms automatically.

Customer inquiry → AI-drafted reply → human approval

An AI drafts the reply; a human approves before anything sends — speed without losing the final say.
