Before we start

What I need from you.

The usual short list for a direct-hire arrangement — having this ready on day one gets work moving faster.

  • 1
    Brand basicsLogo, colors and any existing content, if you have them.
  • 2
    AccessStore, social or workflow logins — shared through a password manager where possible.
  • 3
    A short briefThe goal, the audience, and the deadline — a few sentences is enough to start.
  • 4
    One communication channelEmail, Telegram or Slack, plus a rough expected response window.
  • 5
    Working hours agreementYour timezone and mine (GMT+8), and how often you want updates.
  • 6
    Payment termsAgreed rate and structure — milestone-based or an upfront portion for project work.
